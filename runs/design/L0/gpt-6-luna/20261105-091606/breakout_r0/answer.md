The first hidden layer encodes the ball’s and paddle’s centered horizontal positions. The second layer computes their signed difference: the policy moves toward the ball when they are apart and strongly prefers no-op when aligned. This reactive rule avoids relying on a trajectory estimate that can become misleading after collisions.
