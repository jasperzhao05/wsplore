I use a reflected-trajectory controller. Units 0–3 encode scaled target-minus-paddle errors for the four motion directions. Early targets favor catches that preserve horizontal direction; descending targets in rows 6–8 become midpoints of the valid catch columns. Ascending predictions assume the earliest possible brick bounce, or the ceiling when above the bricks, and update as the ball climbs.

Unit 4 estimates horizontal direction with wall corrections. Unit 5 estimates vertical direction, treating stationary row-8 frames as paddle bounces. The second layer selects the applicable left/right pair, with an approximately 0.75-cell deadband. Outputs strongly prefer movement outside that deadband and no-op otherwise. Unlisted parameters remain zero.
