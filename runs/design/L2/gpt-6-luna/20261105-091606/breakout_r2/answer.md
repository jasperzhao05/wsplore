Ascending projections assume the nearest brick row above the ball will cause a bounce, with a ceiling fallback. Units 4 and 5 infer horizontal and vertical direction from the ball trail, including wall and paddle reflection frames. The second layer gates each projection by direction and turns its signed error into a left/right choice; the output layer favors no-op when the paddle is already suitably placed.
