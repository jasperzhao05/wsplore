# Breakout Policy Network Design

## Strategy Overview

The policy follows a two-tier approach:
1. **Simple tracking**: Follow the ball's current column position
2. **Predictive positioning**: When the ball moves downward, predict landing position based on diagonal trajectory

**Layer 1 computes:**
- Unit 0: Ball-paddle horizontal error (direct follow signal)
- Units 1-2: Horizontal and vertical velocity indicators from ball-trail difference
- Unit 3: Down-right landing prediction error (ball_col + 9 - ball_row - paddle_col)
- Unit 4: Down-left landing prediction error (ball_col + ball_row - 9 - paddle_col)

**Layer 2 gates:**
- Units 0-1: Simple follow signals (move right/left based on current difference)
- Units 2-3: Down-right prediction gated by direction (active when moving down+right)
- Units 4-5: Down-left prediction gated by direction (active when moving down+left)

**Output layer** combines these signals: right action from positive error units, left from negative, with small no-op bias when aligned.
