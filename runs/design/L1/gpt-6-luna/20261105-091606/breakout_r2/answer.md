The four first-layer error units encode paddle-relative landing columns: descending-left/right forecasts use the ball’s remaining steps to row 8, while ascending-left/right forecasts project to an early brick bounce and then to row 8. The forecasts include wall reflections, including the one-step pause at a wall in this implementation. Two more units indicate horizontal and vertical direction. The second layer activates only the matching forecast; paired units preserve the error’s sign so the output favors left or right, with a no-op bias when the paddle is already aligned.
