The four main first-layer units encode landing-column errors for down-left, down-right, up-left, and up-right trajectories. Their lookup weights include the walls’ repeated endpoint cells. Upward predictions assume the earliest geometrically possible brick bounce, revising as the ball climbs. Early targets favor preserving horizontal direction; descending targets in rows 6–8 accept either catch column.

Two direction indicators select the appropriate prediction. Two auxiliary units recognize a paddle-bounce frame and correct its possible horizontal reversal. The second layer gates left/right corrections; otherwise, no-op strongly dominates.
